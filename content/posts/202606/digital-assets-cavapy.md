I had the satisfaction of participating in an event promoted by Cavapy and Excelsis S.A, where we talked about the evolution of the digital asset market and the transformations that are redefining the financial sector.

As this market matures, it becomes increasingly important to have platforms capable of delivering scalability, security, governance, and regulatory compliance.

In this context, Oracle plays a critical role in providing the infrastructure and services needed for institutions to innovate with confidence, accelerating the adoption of new technologies and business models.

I thank the organizers and Giuliano Aversano for the invitation and for the opportunity to share perspectives on a topic so relevant to the future of digital markets.

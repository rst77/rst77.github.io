On June 10, 2026, I had the honor of delivering the Aula Magna at Universidad Columbia del Paraguay, sharing a reflection on the future of the software engineer in the age of AI.

It was a great pleasure to exchange experiences with technology students and professionals at such an important moment for our industry.

My thanks to Dean José Alfredo Souberlich Roman, Universidad Columbia del Paraguay, and OUGPY | Oracle User Group Paraguay for the invitation, the excellent organization, and the warm welcome.

Thank you to everyone who participated and contributed to such an enriching conversation.
